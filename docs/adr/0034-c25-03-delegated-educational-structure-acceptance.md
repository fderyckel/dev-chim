- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03
- Accountable owner: François — Project Owner
- Decider: Codex, using the requested Jamie board-review perspective under explicit delegated Project Owner authority
- Supersedes: ADR 0025 C25-03's representative/domain review prerequisite for the bounded synthetic L1 contract only

- Partially superseded by: [ADR 0035](0035-c25-04-delegated-primary-operator-acceptance.md), which closes synthetic C25-04 and replaces the temporary operator/publication prohibition below; all educational meanings and C25-03-R remain binding
- Additionally partially superseded by: [ADR 0039](0039-public-calendar-evidence-for-cf-3-engineering-entry.md),

## Context

C25-03 requires five representative operating perspectives and an educational-structure domain
disposition before educational institutions, units, parentage, sites, terminology, or affiliations
are persisted. The prepared scenario pack supplies technical cases, but no actual representative
reviewer records. C25-02 was separately closed by ADR 0032; that decision did not close C25-03.

François now explicitly requests the equivalent delegated decision for C25-03:

> Use delegated Project Owner authority to complete C25-03 for synthetic L1 implementation.
> Preserve external representative validation before connected workflows, real data, or deployment.

The review uses Jamie as the requested senior school/college board-review persona. Its actual
author and delegated decider is Codex; it does not invent board service, human qualifications, or
five real institution representatives. The authority comes from François's instruction, not the
persona. This ADR records that exception openly and does not require another approval round.

## Decision drivers

- Permit bounded synthetic educational-structure work with a complete domain disposition.
- Cover primary/early-years, secondary, combined education, college, and university contexts.
- Avoid hard-coded country, age-phase, campus, faculty, or department hierarchies.
- Preserve institutional accountability, learner context, stable identity, and historical meaning.
- Keep C25-04 operator publication and C25-05/C25-06 release/data gates separate.
- Retain actual representative validation before any connected or real institutional use.

## Considered options

1. Keep all educational persistence blocked until real representatives respond. This retains the
   original route but does not implement the Project Owner's synthetic delegation.
2. Treat the Jamie persona as five qualified human reviews. Rejected because that would fabricate
   representative evidence and obscure the authority actually delegated.
3. Close the synthetic L1 decision through a complete delegated review, with an explicit retained
   external-validation gate and no operator or public-workflow bypass. Selected.

## Decision

**C25-03 is closed for synthetic L1 on 2026-10-03, disposition `approve`, under delegated Project
Owner authority.** Adopt the completed review's concept catalogue, five context-specific
dispositions, common answers, scenario/migration findings, and implementation admission rules.
No unanswered C25-03 domain decision remains for that included first-slice contract.

### Accepted educational meanings

- `InstitutionalUnit` has a stable tenant-qualified UUID and one code-owned classification:
  `institution` or `organizational_unit`. A name, label, code, site, or structural depth never
  determines that classification. Initial classification is fixed in the first persistence slice.
- Institutions may be independent roots or contained institutions. An ordinary educational unit
  belongs through containment to its nearest containing institution; a nested institution starts
  its own accountability boundary even when two institutions share an operator.
- Canonical educational parentage is a same-tenant acyclic forest with at most one active parent,
  several roots, and no fixed business-type ladder. An unattached draft unit is unresolved, not a
  published independent institution. No universal group root is fabricated.
- Names and localized terminology are presentation; current optional codes have sibling-scoped
  uniqueness, with institution roots using the tenant root scope. All references use exact UUIDs.
- Physical/virtual `Site` identities and many-to-many unit/site associations are separate from
  educational containment and legal/property responsibility.
- Programmes, courses, cohorts, classes, academic calendars, qualifications, accreditation,
  employment, guardianship, reporting, and access remain separately owned meanings.
- Typed affiliations are the future route for matrix relationships. No generic affiliation, shared
  service, or joint-programme persistence is included in this first slice; the review explicitly
  defers those contracts instead of introducing second parents.

### Synthetic implementation eligibility and C25-04 dependency

This closes the C25-03 entry prerequisite within the already authorized Phase 2 sequence. It makes
the unpublished identity foundation of 2.1-D, followed by initial containment, sites, associations,
and terminology in 2.1-E, eligible for bounded private synthetic L1 implementation. It does not
skip applicable predecessor checks or declare an implementation complete. This documentation
change itself adds no application code, module, migration, or stored institution.

While C25-04 is open, implementation must remain **unpublished**:

- allow named synthetic draft registration, profile revision, initial canonical placement, site
  registration/association, explicit terminology selection, and separately authorized exact reads;
- do not persist operator assignments/transfers or permit publication, operational attachment,
  or an alternative "active" status that asserts verified institutional accountability;
- do not inject a default operator, use a legal parent as operator, or copy a containing
  institution's operator into a nested institution;
- reject publication through every path until the C25-04 dependency is accepted and implemented;
  fixture-only operator examples are logical review evidence, not persisted operator approval.

Persistent reparenting, classification change, transfer, merge/split, closure, reopening,
correction, affiliation actions, historical/bulk traversal, public interfaces, and source imports
remain later bounded work. Reparenting requires registered-impact preview and writer revalidation;
neither an empty UI nor the fact that inputs are synthetic removes that requirement.

The complete sequence's published-institution invariant is unchanged: exactly one effective
verified primary legal operator, and explicit containment-based accountability for ordinary units.
C25-03 closure is not permission to weaken that invariant or close C25-04 by implication.

### Retained representative-validation gate: C25-03-R

`C25-03-R` names the external part retained by the user's delegation; it is not a new replacement
for the closed synthetic decision. François owns arranging named representative and educational-
domain validation covering all five contexts. One person may cover several only with recorded
relevant operating experience for each. The actual reviewer records remain empty until obtained.

This gate must close **before the first connected workflow, real data or migration, pilot, or
deployment that uses this structure**, including a connected synthetic L2 workflow. It retains the
packet's 2026-12-15 review date or the first affected boundary, whichever is earlier. Expiry does
not approve a release; it requires explicit renewal/amendment of the review schedule while the
affected boundary remains blocked.

Required evidence is listed in the completed review: named experience, all seven common answers,
all three context-specific answers for each represented context, scenario findings, actual
terminology and responsibility mappings, concerns/conditions, and a disposition. Rejections that
challenge identity, tenant ownership, accountability, canonical parentage, or non-authority must
be resolved through a superseding ADR before that use proceeds. Synthetic tests cannot substitute
for these representative records or for C25-05 usability/accessibility review.

## Plain-English summary

### What this means

The educational model has an approved synthetic foundation across the five education contexts.
It can be developed using fictional, unpublished institutions while real operating validation
remains a prerequisite for connected use and deployment.

### What was agreed

Schools, faculties, departments, and divisions share stable educational identities without a
fixed ladder of names. Sites remain separate. A structural link grants no permission or implicit
calendar, reporting, curriculum, staffing, or legal responsibility.

### Context

A "school" can be an independent institution or a university unit; "college" and "campus" are
similarly context-dependent. Hard-coding labels would force valid contexts into misleading shapes.

### Examples

- A synthetic combined institution has early-years, primary, and secondary divisions on one site.
- A synthetic university and an independently accountable contained college keep distinct
  institution identities; operator persistence remains gated by C25-04.
- Two synthetic colleges share a site without acquiring a common educational or legal parent.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The first educational persistence contract is settled without fabricating representative reviews.
- Stable identity and separate meanings support growth across education contexts and countries.
- The remaining operator and external-review dependencies are explicit and testable.

### Negative

- Approval lacks real representative operating testimony at this stage.
- Draft-only qualification cannot prove publication, operator transfers, or a connected workflow.
- Matrix programmes, affiliations, complex reorganizations, and real time-zone/records policies
  require later bounded work; they cannot be hidden in labels or generic relationships.

## Security, privacy, operability, and migration effects

TM-17/AC-18 and ADRs 0003, 0005, 0007, 0018, and 0023 remain binding. Every resource, relationship,
query, history item, audit/outbox fact, and projection is tenant-qualified and requires trusted
actor/context, current placement, independent module/capability gates, exact concurrency/replay,
and minimal evidence. PostgreSQL remains authoritative; projections and selected nodes grant
nothing. The review defines negative tests for synthetic draft restrictions as well as structural
invariants. No real person, child, registration, address, operational record, or document is used.

Sources A–F remain compare-only. Missing source parents or operators, ambiguous campus/department
rows, aliases, cycles, and unknown dates are reconciled explicitly, never guessed. Named records
and independent security/privacy reviews remain required for later real adoption.

## Fallback and exit cost

If a synthetic case cannot fit without an inference or invented identity, keep it unresolved and
limit implementation to the admitted contract. A contradictory representative finding reopens the
affected meaning through a superseding decision. Before real adoption, the cost is confined to
documents, fixtures, and synthetic code/schema. Any later real change requires preserved IDs,
revisioned mappings, expand-and-contract migration, impact review, and recovery evidence.

## Review triggers

- A represented institution needs two canonical parents or a classification with different behavior.
- A nested institution cannot resolve its own accountable operator under the C25-04 contract.
- A campus, faculty, college, department, group, or network cannot be mapped without invented meaning.
- Joint delivery, academic ownership, accreditation, employment, or learner relationships are being
  forced into containment, labels, or sites.
- A move crosses an accountability/time-zone boundary or changes an explicitly authorized scope.
- A connected workflow, actual representative rejection, migration, real data, or deployment is proposed.
