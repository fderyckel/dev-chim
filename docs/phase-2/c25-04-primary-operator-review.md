## Board-level judgment

Approve the seven rules. The crucial distinction is between the organization recorded as legally
accountable, the current reliability of that evidence, and permission for a particular action.
Combining those into a single “active” flag would conceal a disputed operator or unnecessarily
stop services to learners. Keep identity, lifecycle, verification and action policy separate.

A governance dispute must not casually strand pupils or college students. Conversely, continuing
classes does not establish authority to accept new fee liabilities, grant awards, sponsor visas,
sign contracts or transfer protected records. Treat each effect explicitly. A common brand, board,
shareholder, property owner or educational parent cannot settle those questions.

For college retention, protect both continuity of study and the custody/accessibility of learner
records. Institution closure or operator transfer must not destroy results, transcripts, appeals,
fee liabilities, complaints or safeguarding records. Retention periods and custodians require
their own applicable records policy; no universal duration is invented here.

## Seven requested decisions

| Proposed rule | Decision | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One authorized initial assignment while unpublished | Approve | Named action, exact institution/operator IDs and versions, evidence metadata, policy and impact check; pending verification remains draft. Initial assignment cannot overwrite an existing relationship or erase a publication history |
| Proposal plus separate approval after publication | Approve | Different authorized people, not merely different accounts. Approval binds the exact evidence, proposal, effective date and impact revisions. Unpublishing cannot restore the initial-assignment shortcut |
| Explicit single-controller exception | Approve with settled controls | Tenant policy must allow it; verified fresh step-up assurance, urgency/necessity and conflict reasons, protected reference, visible marker, independent reviewer and alternate, deadline and escalation are all mandatory. A sole shareholder is not automatically a sole qualified controller |
| Revalidation at the effective boundary | Approve | Evidence applicability/status, current authorizations, both entities, expected versions, approved impacts and policy are checked by the writer; stale or unknown input blocks. Approval alone never activates |
| Revocation opens accountability under review | Approve | Immediate durable case, restrictions and alert obligation; original intervals and discovery/recorded times remain. Do not substitute an operator, rewrite history, unpublish or change authority implicitly |
| Essential services continue subject to policy | Approve | Explicit action classification and ordinary authorization; applicable legal/safeguarding suspension overrides continuity. Unknown classification blocks. Protect existing learner support without permitting new legally dependent commitments |
| Four resolution routes only | Approve | Reverify, approved transfer, attributable factual correction, or separately authorized suspension/closure. Each route needs its own evidence and impacts; no generic dismissal or automatic clearance |

“Approve with settled controls” in the exception row describes an incorporated rule refinement,
not a pending review condition. The overall gate disposition is **approve** for synthetic L1.

## Exact values and accountable assignments

| Item | Accepted value |
| --- | --- |
| Accountable owner | **Tenant compliance/governance owner**; named individual through tenant-defined duties/capabilities |
| Retrospective review | **Complete within 14 calendar days of exception authorization** |
| Revocation response | **Immediate alert obligation, case and restrictions; human reconciliation initiated within 72 elapsed hours of credible discovery** |

The compliance/governance owner must have authority to require evidence, assign an independent
reviewer, escalate to the governing body and prevent further exception use. The actual reviewer
must be distinct from the exception actor and free of the relevant conflict. If the owner made
the exceptional decision, the preassigned independent alternate or a qualified external reviewer
performs review. If no independent reviewer is assigned, the exception is unavailable. These are
duty descriptions, not built-in production role names. François owns the project decision, not
an invented appointment to every future tenant's governing body.

Start the 14-day clock when the exception authorization commits, even for a future transfer.
Use the stored institution IANA zone and the same local time 14 calendar dates later, with
nonexistent times advanced to the first valid instant and ambiguous times using the earlier
instant. Store the resulting UTC deadline. UTC synthetic fixtures avoid unresolved locale
assumptions. Resubmission or reassignment cannot reset the obligation. A future transfer whose
review is already due cannot activate with that review outstanding.

Review completion records necessity, actor authority/assurance, conflicts, evidence applicability,
impacts and continuity, with one finding: uphold, uphold with remediation, or reject. Any unmet
activation prerequisite in a remediation finding blocks activation; it is not conditional legal
validity. An adverse finding after activation opens/updates accountability under review. An
overdue review prevents further exception use and blocks its pending activation, escalates to the
named governing-body chair/designated executive, and places an already effective exception under
accountability review. It never automatically rolls back the operator or shuts down teaching.

The 72-hour clock runs continuously from earliest recorded credible discovery. Restrictions and
the case are committed immediately, with a durable outbox alert delivered after commit; delivery
failure must remain visible and retryable. Human initiation means a named investigator has
recorded initial findings, affected actions, learner continuity and a dated remediation/resolution
plan. A notification acknowledgement or automatically opened case is insufficient. Owner review
then occurs at least every 24 elapsed hours until resolution. Missed plan/deadline milestones
escalate without clearing restrictions. A higher-risk emergency or applicable shorter deadline
requires faster action; neither default is permission to delay necessary protection.

The **14 days / 72 hours are synthetic defaults**, expressly replacing the earlier C25-02 Q8–Q10
recommendations of two business/five calendar days and four-/24-hour responses for this contract.
They do not claim equivalence to any country's mandatory periods. Immediate protective controls
are the reason these longer administrative outer limits are acceptable for synthetic proof.

## Evidence types, freshness and revocation

| Evidence class | What it can establish | Synthetic verification and invalidation rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official entity register, incorporation record or status certificate | Exact legal identity and applicable entity status | Obtain a status check no older than 30 calendar days at proposal/initial verification and refresh at publication/activation; a certificate alone does not authorize school operation |
| Applicable statute, charter, licence, registration or regulator order | Authority to operate this institution within the specified jurisdiction/scope | Record operative version, issuer, institution/entity coverage, conditions, commencement and expiry; verify current applicability at publication/activation. Revocation, suspension, expiry, supersession or unmet conditions block reliance |
| Executed operating/transfer instrument plus required approvals | A contractual appointment or transfer where the applicable reviewed policy recognizes that route | Verify execution, parties, scope, effective date, termination and all conditions precedent. A contract or internal board resolution cannot substitute for required public authority |
| Board resolution, delegation instrument or authorized signatory record | Authority of the people approving the decision | Verify current delegation, limits and conflicts at approval and activation; it does not by itself prove the operator's licence or appointment |
| Protected verification attestation and status check | Who examined which version/source, when, under what policy, with what result | Record verifier, timestamp, policy/version, protected reference, applicable dates, classification and outcome; verify against the underlying instruments, not a bare assertion |
| Court/regulator/interim appointment instrument | A time-bounded legally accountable interim organization, where applicable | Verify actual mandate and expiry; use the normal legal-entity operator interval. An appointed person remains a person/authority record, not a fabricated legal entity |

These are exact synthetic evidence classifications, not a claim that each instrument exists or
suffices in every country. A versioned evidence policy maps each applicable institution/operator
jurisdiction and evidence type to required instruments and checks. Unknown mapping fails closed.
The governance owner owns the mapping with the qualified jurisdictional reviewer before real use;
the records owner owns document access, retention, legal hold, export and disposal. No full
document or secret-bearing URL enters structure, audit, events, logs, errors or prompts.

The 30-day value applies to synthetic status-check freshness, not to the age of a valid founding
instrument. Explicit expiry or shorter requirements win. At activation/publication, perform and
record the current check again; do not reuse an earlier “verified” flag. Synthetic fixtures
exercise the policy using fabricated instruments and deterministic checks, never real documents.
Fresh stronger assurance means trusted proof under ADR 0029's qualified policy, bound to the
actor and action; an arbitrary client claim or boolean “MFA passed” is insufficient. If that
policy cannot establish required assurance, the exception fails closed.

Credible revocation/invalidation events include formal withdrawal or suspension, operative expiry,
termination, superseding orders, failed conditions, wrong institution/jurisdiction coverage,
forgery or erroneous verification. Loss of document access is an inability to reverify, not proof
of revocation, but blocks activation and opens review if current standing cannot be supported.
Record occurrence date separately from discovery/recorded time. A suspected problem is visibly
under review; confirming or disproving it requires attributable evidence.

## Effective time and impact review

Each published institution has one recorded active primary operator; one legal entity may operate
several institutions. A separately accountable contained institution has its own relationship.
Ordinary units resolve through the nearest containing institution and do not receive copied links.
Recorded assignment and present evidence status remain distinct during an under-review incident.

Apply the accepted inclusive-start/exclusive-end institution-local date contract. A transfer
atomically closes the predecessor and opens the successor, with no overlaps/gaps introduced by
partial writes. Preserve UTC approval, discovery and commit times. Source requirements needing
intra-day legal precision remain unresolved; no rounding or guessed historic interval is allowed.
A failed activation leaves recorded history intact but must not assert the predecessor remains
legally valid if its own evidence expired: open accountability review where necessary.

The exact impact preview names finance, contracts, staffing/payroll, safeguarding, records and
custody, admissions, programmes/awards, student support, workflows, reporting, integrations,
authority scopes and module data. Registered consumers attest to the proposal and versions;
unknown consumers or unclassified effects block. Required notifications, consent, contract
novation, records transfers or regulator approvals are explicit prerequisites/outcomes, never
inferred from the operator edge. A later change invalidates the affected preview and approval.
No external side effect precedes a committed domain decision.

## Continuity and permitted resolutions

| Action group | Under-review treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Existing teaching, attendance, safeguarding and learner support | Continue only under explicit continuity policy, with ordinary authorization and any applicable suspension instruction enforced; never disable safeguarding reporting merely because finance/ownership is disputed |
| Existing learner records, transcripts, complaints and completion support | Preserve records, custody and authorized access; distinguish access to existing results from authority to confer a new award; protect teach-out/transfer options and applicable holds |
| New admissions/fee commitments, contracts, external financial/legal claims, new awards or sponsorship | Block when verified operator standing is required; any narrower permitted case requires its own reviewed policy and evidence |
| Payroll, essential payments, data disclosure and records transfer | Require an explicit domain continuity decision and named owner; neither blanket permission nor automatic suspension follows from the operator link |
| Unknown or newly introduced action | Fail closed until classified and reviewed; no broad emergency or “learner support” override |

Reverification establishes current applicability with fresh evidence. Approved transfer establishes
a verified successor and reconciles the disputed period without pretending it never happened.
Factual correction appends an ADR 0018 correction with evidence and downstream reconciliation;
correcting a date or typo alone cannot clear an unrelated current defect. Suspension/closure
requires its separate authority, impact review and continuity/records obligations; it resolves
the operating disposition, not by labelling invalid evidence valid or erasing remaining duties.
Each route closes a case only after its required current-state and consequence checks succeed;
remaining obligations stay tracked with owners even when institution lifecycle changes.

Required implementation evidence includes positive cases and all of these negative cases:

1. Missing/stale actor or tenant, foreign-tenant institution/entity/evidence/case, unavailable module,
   stale placement or absent capability denies without disclosure or partial writes.
2. Repeat initial assignment, “unpublish then reassign,” self-approval by another account, stale
   proposal/approval and changed effective date cannot bypass the workflow.
3. Exception without any one required control, independent reviewer/alternate, valid assurance,
   or with a prior overdue review fails; future activation after a missed review is blocked.
4. LS-N17/LS-N18: missing/revoked/expired/inaccessible evidence, unknown policy, changed entity status,
   lost approval authority, stale versions or changed/unclassified impacts block activation.
5. Racing transfers, publication and revocation serialize; replay produces no duplicate intervals,
   cases, review obligations or outbox facts. Rollback leaves no partial history or side effect.
6. Deadline fixtures cover weekends, future activation, DST gaps/repeated times, reassignment,
   rejection, overdue escalation and exactly-at-deadline transitions. No clock resets or auto-clear.
7. LS-N19: revocation preserves history, immediately creates restrictions and durable alerts, denies
   standing-dependent/unknown actions, and permits only explicitly classified continuity actions.
8. Alert delivery failure remains retryable and visible; acknowledgement alone fails the 72-hour
   requirement. A dated plan leaves restrictions in place until successful resolution.
9. LS-N20: generic dismiss, silent rollback, automatic closure, unverified successor, fake legal
   entity for a natural person and unsupported correction cannot clear accountability review.
10. Documents, protected references and confidential incident details do not leak through events,
    logs, errors, lists, unrelated tenant reads or unauthorized deadline/alert recipients.

These are acceptance requirements, not claims that these workflows or tests already exist.
