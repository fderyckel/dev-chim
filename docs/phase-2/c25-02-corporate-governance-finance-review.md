## Board-level assessment

The model is suitable as a deliberately small structural foundation. A board should be able to
distinguish the institution educating a learner, the organization legally operating it, the
employer paying staff, the owner of its buildings, the funding or contracting party, and the body
exercising a specific governance right. None can safely be substituted for another.

Illustrative analytical cases, not claims of personal experience or country-specific approval:

- A private school group uses an operating company and a separate property company. Rent,
  employment, school operation, and equity are different responsibilities; a building-owner edge
  must not appoint a school operator or confer safeguarding authority.
- A foundation has a right to appoint some college governors but holds no shares. The right is
  representable without inventing equity or claiming that the foundation controls every decision.
- A separately operated college sits inside a university's educational organization. Its operator
  and the university's educational parentage remain independent; the label "college" settles
  neither legal status nor accountability.
- A cross-border group uses one management overview, while the applicable legal and accounting
  reporting perimeters require separate assessment. The overview must not be labelled a verified
  consolidated financial statement.
- A brand, accreditation, management-services agreement, funding arrangement, or shared board
  member does not by itself establish the specific legal power represented by an included type.

The accepted catalogue does not model beneficial owners, natural-person trustees, every trust's
legal personality, joint arrangements, minority protections, branches, guarantees, or complete
finance. An unsupported case remains explicitly outside scope, not falsely represented. The
one-primary-operator invariant remains a product contract subject to C25-03/C25-04 validation for
the actual institution; it is not asserted to be a universal rule of education law.

## First-slice relationship catalogue

The following common rules and the two tables together supply all eleven requested fields for
every included entry. Corporate membership and parentage are logical relationships; their names
do not require separate resources or independently callable actions in C1.

### Common accepted rules

1. Every endpoint and evidence reference is tenant-qualified. All included direct relationships
   connect distinct `LegalEntity` identities. Natural persons, sites, educational units, and
   corporate units are not substitutes for those endpoints.
2. Direct relationships record one evidenced right or interest and its exact basis; the existence
   of a right is not a conclusion about overall legal or accounting control. A duplicate assertion
   is not an additional economic holding. Conflicting assertions remain unresolved; percentages
   across edges must not be added or multiplied by the platform.
3. Effective intervals are inclusive-start/exclusive-end Dates; recorded time is separate UTC.
   Synthetic C1 uses an explicit UTC fixture calendar for legal/corporate Date interpretation. It
   cannot borrow an institution's time zone: these relationships may exist without any institution.
   No instant-to-Date conversion or real cross-jurisdiction temporal claim is authorized. Actual
   instrument-specific calendar/time-zone and instant precision require later adoption validation.
4. Every record has its type, directional endpoints, effective basis, classified synthetic
   evidence reference, and attributable named action. The reference resolves the asserted basis
   in the review fixture; it is not a free-text legal finding. No names, registrations, legal
   documents, personal information, or secrets belong in that reference. Production documents,
   if later authorized, belong in an approved protected records system with separate access,
   retention, hold, and erasure controls.
5. In C1, the appropriate current tenant-defined capability and all module gates authorize a
   named establishment/registration action. "Trusted actor" alone is insufficient. This records
   synthetic facts; it neither executes a legal instrument nor certifies its validity. Separate
   real-world approvals in the second table are future adoption obligations, not invented C1
   workflows or fixed application roles.
6. Across every type: no inferred access, operation, legal personality, financial obligation,
   accounting treatment, report membership, tax status, configuration, module activation, or
   placement. A path cannot establish indirect control. Unknown or unrepresented means unknown,
   not zero or absent. Reconciliation reviews downstream effects; it never changes them implicitly.
7. Self-links are excluded from this first slice, including treasury/self-holding cases. Distinct-
   entity reciprocal and longer cycles are admitted for the four direct types only as separate
   evidenced assertions; this is a representational choice, not a claim that a cycle is lawful.
   Management and corporate parentage prohibit all cycles, including concurrent cycles.

## Thirteen corporate-governance and finance answers

1. **Distinguish ownership/control, consolidation, and operation — yes.** Accept only the precise
   direct meanings above. Management parentage supplies a chosen view, not an accounting
   perimeter. Primary operation belongs to the institution's separate relationship.

2. **Essential first-slice types — settled.** Include the four ADR 0031 direct types and the three
   structural meanings in the catalogue. Do not include operator, educational, or site relations.
   The common rules and both tables settle every required field; none depends on a future generic
   "ownership/control" interpretation.

3. **Cycles — yes, type-specific.** Cross-holdings and reciprocal scoped rights can be represented
   as direct assertions without any inference from a path. Allow those for the four direct types,
   excluding self-links. Prohibit management and corporate containment cycles. Displaying or
   storing a cycle never certifies legal validity or calculates indirect control.

4. **One optional management parent — yes for the bounded purpose.** Several roots are legitimate.
   This cannot support statutory consolidation, joint arrangements, accounting eliminations,
   minority interests, currency translation, or several reporting frameworks by itself. A
   separate finance-owned assessment is required before any such use.

5. **Owned jurisdiction/evidence policies — yes with explicit admission limits.** Deferral is
   defensible because C1 has no real data. It does not make registration, legal personality,
   closure, freshness, or correction requirements optional when adopted. Unsupported legal forms,
   evidence, equity bases, or timing are excluded from C1. Later adoption must name the applicable
   jurisdiction, legal/records owner, and policy before accepting real records.

6. **Changes requiring approval/reconciliation — specified by consequence.** An operator change
   requires the C25-04 process. Title or lease changes require property-owner review; employment
   moves require employment-owner review; funding/contract changes require finance and contract
   owner review; appointment powers require governance review; data-control changes require the
   responsible privacy/records owners. An internal move does not novate any of those obligations.
   Impact review identifies a responsible owner and exact affected records; unclassified effects
   block the consequential action. These are later actions, not additions to C1.

7. **Date precision — sufficient only for date-based evidence.** Operator changes use the
   institution's stored IANA time zone under ADR 0025, with separate UTC approval/recording time.
   A legally significant midday transfer cannot be rounded. Source F must remain unresolved
   without a proven effective boundary. Legal/corporate C1 facts use the explicit synthetic UTC
   calendar in the common rules, not an invented institution reference. Exact-time real evidence
   remains outside this contract.

8. **Initial assignment, separate approval, and single-controller exception — proportionate as a
   later workflow proposal.** Single-step initial assignment is acceptable while unpublished;
   publication still needs verified evidence. A published transfer normally needs different
   proposer and approver actors. "Single controller" means only one qualified application
   decision-maker is available, not that the entity has one shareholder. Share-transfer documents
   do not justify bypassing approval.

   Recommended C25-04 baseline: name an independent retrospective reviewer before the exception;
   complete review within **two business days and no later than five calendar days** after effect.
   The tenant's named Legal Accountability Owner is responsible and reports overdue cases to the
   governing-body chair or designated accountable executive. These are duties mapped to
   tenant-defined capabilities, not production role constants. An appropriately appointed external
   reviewer can supply independence if the tenant has no second qualified person. If nobody can
   review, the exception is unavailable. Require step-up assurance, exact proposal/evidence/version
   binding, urgency and conflict rationale, proof of legal operator authority, visible exception
   status, and a review obligation established before activation. An overdue case escalates and
   prevents further exception use pending resolution; it does not silently undo the operator or
   halt essential education. C25-04 must qualify this workflow before implementation.

9. **Evidence, freshness, revocation, and records boundary — use validity plus verification.**
   Candidate evidence is type-specific: share register and completed transfer/issuance;
   governing instrument and amendments; applicable law/formal act; executed contract and operative
   conditions; internal management instruction; and, for a future operator, the applicable
   authorization, licence, agreement, or public instrument naming that operator.

   Do not require every document to have been issued in the last 30 days: an older operative
   instrument can remain relevant. Recommended later-adoption baseline: status/extract checks
   verified within **30 calendar days** of proposal, or a shorter source/legal validity limit;
   instruments checked for amendment, expiry, termination, and applicable conditions at proposal
   and again before effect. A future transfer revalidates at activation regardless of the age of
   approval. Missing authoritative access is unresolved, not proof of revocation. Revocation
   triggers include a relevant suspension/withdrawal, court or regulator order, expiry,
   termination, superseding instrument, failed condition, or evidence found inapplicable/false.
   Deregistration/insolvency is assessed for its actual legal effect rather than universally
   erasing every right. Store only necessary classified metadata and a protected reference;
   document contents remain in the approved records system. Events must not carry evidence
   references or percentages under ADR 0031.

10. **Ownership and response time — explicit later operating baseline.** The tenant must appoint
    a named Legal Accountability Owner, backed by jurisdiction-competent legal advice, to own
    evidence validity and reconciliation. A named Finance Reporting Owner owns accounting use;
    records and operational continuity have their own accountable owners. Until those appointments
    and capabilities exist, real adoption remains prohibited. François owns this design decision,
    not fictitious tenant appointments.

    On detection of credible invalidation: flag accountability under review, preserve history,
    alert the accountable owner, and block affected/unknown operator-dependent actions immediately
    through the later qualified workflow. Recommended response: **acknowledgement within four
    elapsed hours; documented triage and continuity decision within 24 elapsed hours; verified
    resolution or an approved, dated remediation plan within two business days**, with daily review
    while unresolved. A legal order or imminent safeguarding concern takes priority immediately.
    No universal 72-hour grace period applies before restrictions. An accepted plan does not clear
    the unresolved condition. These are explicit design recommendations for C25-04 adoption, not
    asserted cross-country legal deadlines or a claim that a monitor exists.

11. **Standing-dependent versus essential actions — keep a reviewed action matrix.** New operator-
    dependent attestations, operator changes, regulatory submissions, and commitments requiring
    that standing must not proceed on disputed evidence. Teaching, safeguarding, attendance, and
    learner support should continue where lawful; payroll and essential payments require an
    explicit continuity decision, not blanket blocking or blanket permission. Unknown actions fail
    closed. Applicable suspension orders must be honoured through the separately authorized
    process. No actual jurisdiction's suspension rule is certified by this synthetic review.

12. **Resolution set — adequate for the bounded contract.** Reverify, approved transfer, ADR 0018
    correction, or separate suspension/closure are the permitted substantive outcomes. Every
    outcome must resolve the disputed fact and applicable restrictions, not merely clear a flag.
    An interim legally accountable organization uses a time-bounded operator relationship; an
    appointed individual uses a separate person/authority contract. If joint operators or a legal
    form cannot fit, block that adoption and supersede the affected model; do not invent an entity
    or select an arbitrary primary operator.

13. **Sequence and claims — yes.** C1 is a small structural register with exact reads. It is not a
    company registry, operational school module, accounting consolidation engine, or complete
    governance system. This review closes C25-02's logical decision; executable qualification and
    C25-03 through C25-06 remain separate.

The numerical recommendations in answers 8–10 are transparent design judgements, not statutory
limits. They settle this review's recommendations without purporting to close C25-04 or impose an
unimplemented workflow. Its accountable owner must validate staffing, source access, jurisdiction,
continuity, and enforcement before that workflow is authorized.
