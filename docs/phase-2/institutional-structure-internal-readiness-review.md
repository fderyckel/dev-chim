## Readiness findings

### IR-01 — future-effective transfer execution

The logical rule is acceptable, but later implementation must not assume an undeclared scheduler.
A future transfer remains pending until one named activation action runs on the authoritative
writer at or after its boundary and revalidates evidence, entity status, approvals, expected
versions, and registered impacts. The L1 proof may invoke that action directly. Any automatic
timer, worker, or scheduler requires its separately authorized operating contract. A late
activation must preserve the intended effective date and recorded time distinctly and trigger
explicit reconciliation for decisions made from the previously current view.

Disposition: implementation condition; not an L0 logical blocker.

### IR-02 — accountability review is not a second authority engine

The accountability-under-review condition is a legal-standing domain fact. A later module that
legally depends on verified operator standing must declare a code-owned dependency and action
classification, then recheck the current condition on the authoritative writer. The condition
does not grant or revoke actor capabilities. An undeclared dependency fails closed for the
operator-dependent action; it does not create a generic tenant-wide policy language.

Disposition: implementation condition and cross-module acceptance check; not an L0 logical
blocker.

### IR-03 — temporal relationship constraints

Corporate parentage is valid only across the overlap of both units' effective membership in the
same legal entity. Operator, membership, parentage, and consolidation changes need interval-aware
serialization and constraints; checking only the effective start date is insufficient. The
existing alternate-write, concurrency, and impact-preview requirements are the correct proof
location.

Disposition: L1 executable condition; not an L0 logical blocker.

### IR-04 — current prototype coverage

The route has a strong read-only boundary, clear legal/corporate/educational separation, textual
non-colour outcomes, semantic headings and nested lists, stable selected-unit context, and usable
wide/narrow reflow. The 2026-09-27 refresh closes the four technical findings by:

1. showing the operator proposal, distinct approval or single-controller exception, evidence
   verification, effective-boundary revalidation, accountability-under-review, and named
   resolution meanings;
2. exposing all five representative perspectives in the review experience through an
   inspectable fixed fixture;
3. showing at least one permitted or ready preview as well as blocked previews so participants can
   distinguish governance from permanent unavailability; and
4. testing deeper-tree indentation, wide/narrow and 720-pixel 200%-equivalent reflow, focus order,
   semantic structure, and automated accessibility after those additions.

Disposition: technical G5 ready; representative assistive-technology/comprehension review remains
C25-05, and all G2 perspective findings remain C25-03.

### IR-05 — domain adequacy cannot be self-certified

The first legal relationship catalogue, jurisdictional evidence/freshness rules, finance meaning,
records policy, and the five operating-context terminology mappings still require the named human
reviews in the packet. Repository authorship, product ownership, synthetic scenarios, web
research, or this internal challenge cannot substitute for them.

Disposition: retained as C25-02 through C25-06 at the first affected slice or release.
