# Quality-target approval evidence

- Status: Approval required
- Owner: Product and operations owners
- Review date: DATE_REQUIRED

Approve the numeric targets in [quality-attribute targets](../../architecture/quality-attribute-targets.md). Record the target workload, burst window, write and storage amplification, mixed reporting load, retention, environment, measurement method, accountable owner, and review date. Do not approve placeholder or subjective targets. Student count or annual row count without a time distribution is not a capacity target.
